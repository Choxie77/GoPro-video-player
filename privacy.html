Privacy – GoPro Travels Player

A small personal page where friends and family watch Adam's travel clips. Not a business, nothing is sold or shared.

What signing in with Google does

You sign in with your Google account. The page asks for read-only access to Google Drive, only so your browser can play the video clips stored there. Your Google token stays in your browser and is not stored on any server of mine.

What is stored

If you comment or watch a clip, a small private spreadsheet in my own Google account records your Google name and email, your comment, and which clips were viewed and when. Only I can see it. It is used to show comments and see which clips people watched.

What I do not do

I do not read, copy or change anything in your Drive, I do not sell or share your data, and I do not use it for ads.

Removing your data

Revoke access any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To have your comments and records deleted, email alovrencak@gmail.com.
